# Before and after

> **This is a fictional example.** "Priya", her employers and all figures are made up to show
> what the tool does. The kinds of problems it finds are the ones it finds on real profiles.

**Priya** is a backend engineer with six years' experience who wants to move into **Senior
Platform Engineer** roles. She shares her LinkedIn PDF and two job postings.

## What the audit found

- Her three strongest results — cutting cloud costs by 35%, a migration of 40 services to
  Kubernetes, and an on-call alert reduction — appeared **only in About**. Her Experience section
  listed duties, so recruiters skimming roles never saw them.
- The same role was listed **twice** (an internal transfer entered as a new job), and the skill
  "Kubernetes" appeared under two spellings.
- A cloud certification had **expired**, but About still said "certified".
- Her Skills section was at LinkedIn's **100-skill limit**, with generic tags like "Teamwork" and
  "Microsoft Office", so relevant skills like Terraform couldn't be added.
- Her most recent title was "Software Developer II", which doesn't match the platform roles she's
  targeting.

## Headline

**Before** (95 characters)
> Software Developer II at Northwind | Passionate about technology | Problem solver | Team player

**After** (154 characters)
> Platform Engineer | Kubernetes, Terraform & AWS | Migrated 40 services to Kubernetes and cut cloud costs 35% | CI/CD, observability & developer experience

Why: it leads with the target title, gets the top three skills into the first 50 characters,
drops the buzzwords, and adds a concrete proof point.

## An experience bullet

**Before**
> Responsible for maintaining CI/CD pipelines and supporting the infrastructure team.

**After** (once the tool asked for the real figures)
> Rebuilt the CI/CD pipeline on GitHub Actions for 40 services, cutting average build time from
> 18 to 6 minutes and letting teams deploy daily instead of weekly.

## About — first line

**Before**
> I am a passionate and results-driven software developer with a strong interest in cloud
> technologies and a proven ability to work in fast-paced environments…

**After**
> I help engineering teams ship faster and spend less on the cloud — by building platforms that
> make the right way the easy way.

## The fill-in loop

The first draft left placeholders such as `[X] services migrated` and `build time from [A] to [B]
minutes`. Priya answered in rough notes ("40 services", "about 18 min down to 6", "no idea on
exact savings, it was roughly a third"), and the final version used exactly those facts — the
cost figure became "about 35%", nothing guessed.
